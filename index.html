PRJ_012

Lensless Imaging for Airborne Carbor Fiber Detection

Developing a low-cost lensless holographic imaging system to detect and distinguish airborne carbon fiber fragments from conventional dust particles generated during composite manufacturing, whereby computational imaging and Fourier-based analysis to classify particles based on their diffraction patterns is being explored. The goal is to improve workplace safety by providing an accessible method for identifying potentially hazardous airborne fibers.

Optics & SensingSustainabilityDesign Analysis & Optimization
PRJ_013

Origami-Inspired Robot for Confined-Space Navigation

Designing and developing a compact, shape-adaptive robot capable of navigating confined spaces through origami-inspired structures and compliant mechanisms. The project investigates folding geometries, flexible enclosures, and locomotion systems that allow the robot to actively compress its profile, maintain mobility, and recover its original configuration. The goal is to develop a versatile robotic platform for applications in search-and-rescue operations, infrastructure inspection, and other environments with limited accessibility.
